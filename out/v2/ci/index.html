Build, test, and deploy with confidence

Forgewright is an open-source self-hosted CI/CD pipeline runner designed for engineering teams who need control, reliability, and performance.

Key Features

Self-hosted CI/CD

Run your pipelines anywhere, with full control over your infrastructure and data.

Open-source & customizable

Open-source code with a modular architecture that can be extended to fit your team's needs.

High performance

Optimized for fast builds and reliable execution, with support for parallel processing and distributed runs.

How it works

1

FAQ

What is Forgewright?
Forgewright is an open-source self-hosted CI/CD pipeline runner designed for engineering teams who need control, reliability, and performance.
Is Forgewright free to use?
Yes, Forgewright is open-source and free to use. You can host it on your own infrastructure or use our hosted version.
Can I customize Forgewright?
Absolutely. Forgewright is designed to be highly customizable with a modular architecture that supports custom plugins and integrations.
