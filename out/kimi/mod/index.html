FAQ

Billing questions, answered plainly.

Something else on your mind? Talk to us — a human replies within a few hours.

What counts as a monitor?

One URL, IP, or heartbeat endpoint you check. A single site with 3 pages checked separately counts as 3 monitors.

Can I switch between monthly and annual billing?

Yes, anytime from Settings → Billing. Switching to annual applies the 20% discount immediately with prorated credit for the remainder of your current month.

What happens if I go over my SMS allowance on Pro?

We never cut off alerts mid-incident. Extra SMS messages are billed at $0.10 each, and we'll email you when you hit 80% of the included allowance.

Do you offer refunds?

Annual plans can be cancelled within 30 days for a full refund. After that, cancellation stops renewal — no partial refunds, and your plan stays active until the period ends.

Is the free plan really free?

Yes — no credit card required, no trial clock. 10 monitors and email alerts forever.
